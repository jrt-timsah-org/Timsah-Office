- **CoRE rule text**: official CoRE-2 rule navigator V27.2.0, https://core.scramble-robot.org/rule/core-2-rulenavi/ ; common and competition-system rulebooks v26.1.0, https://github.com/scramble-robot/CoRE-Rulebook . Local text snapshots support offline retrieval and include original URLs, version/revision, acquisition time and SHA-256. Normative blocks are indexed; explanatory summaries, the original navigator's JavaScript, fonts, CAD geometry and third-party trackers are not included. Diagrams and linked content must be checked in the original. All original ownership is retained. See `data/rules/snapshot.json`.

Weights are unmodified and no fine-tuning has been performed. Original license: `licenses/Qwen3.LICENSE`. https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B , https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B , https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3-0.6B-GGUF , https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3-1.7B-GGUF .
